1) Which sections of the website are restricted for crawling?
- For User-agent: * (general rule) The restrictions of these sections
- /w/
- /api/
- /trap/
    
2) Are there specific rules for certain user agents?
- Yes. Many named bots are disallowed entirely (disallowed: /)
- Some bots have special rules, crawl-delay

3) Why sites use robots.txt
It lets site owners communicate what part of the site auto crawlers should and should not access.
It helps prevent excessive load on servers and reduce crawling of dynamic and sensitive areas.
Following these guidelines respects the site owner's published rules.
